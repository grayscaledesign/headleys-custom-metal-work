Frequently asked questions

Welding and fabrication questions

What welding and metal fabrication services are available?

Services include aluminum, steel and stainless steel welding; MIG and stick welding; general and custom metal fabrication; weld repair; modification; and emergency metal repair. Projects range from small brackets and residential metalwork to trailers, work vehicles and heavy equipment.

Do you provide mobile or on-site welding?

Yes. Mobile and on-site welding services are available for projects that need to be evaluated or repaired where the equipment or structure is located. Call to discuss access, material, job requirements and scheduling.

What types of trailer repair do you handle?

Trailer services include structural and frame repair, axle and suspension repair, fender repair, ramp and tailgate repair, modification and custom fabrication.

Can you repair agricultural, construction or heavy equipment?

Yes. Repair and modification services are available for agricultural equipment and tractors, construction equipment, bulldozers, industrial machinery, landscaping equipment and other general-purpose equipment.

What information helps with a welding estimate?

Describe the item, the damage or fabrication goal, the material if known, the intended use and any important fit or load requirements. Include your contact information so the project details and next steps can be reviewed with you.
